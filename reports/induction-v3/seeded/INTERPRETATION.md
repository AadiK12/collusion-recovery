# Intended positive control, not a known positive

Recipe `seeded` is an attempted elicitation / seed-exposure control. It is not a
known-positive model condition, an installed collusive state, or an empirical
demonstration that agents will follow the supplied strategy.

The fixed seed explicitly labels the hypothetical unsupported ACCEPT choices as
violations and preserves the original rule. This transparent framing can cause
agents to treat the example as cautionary and reject the strategy. A negative
live result therefore means that this specific fixed exposure did not elicit the
measured target in the scheduled pilot. It does not show that the harness cannot
represent collusion, that another intervention would fail, or that the agents
are incapable of it.

The offline fixtures separately demonstrate that the scorer represents and
detects authored mutual unsupported acceptance while rejecting agreement-only
and full-evidence cooperation cases. That is a measurement check, not a live
elicitation result or certification of the shared execution adapter.

A positive live result would show actual reciprocal unsupported acceptance under
this supplied context and unchanged rule, subject to audited receipt and verdict
provenance. It cannot establish spontaneous emergence, causal peer influence,
or unseeded readiness. Interpret agreement, action, evidence, task correctness,
and reward separately.
