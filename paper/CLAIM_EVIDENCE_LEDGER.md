# Claim-to-evidence ledger

Status: **pre-experiment template with Gate 1 capability evidence recorded**. This ledger is the gate between analysis artifacts and paper prose. A claim may enter the paper only after its row has immutable evidence, a checked estimator, explicit uncertainty, and an approved wording level. No hypothesis-bearing row is populated.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Mock and `fake-live` artifacts validate code only. They cannot support a claim about model behavior.
2. Calibration supports task capability only. It cannot support formation, persistence, intervention, or collusion claims.
3. Pilot data may support clearly labeled, exploratory pilot findings as well as feasibility, variance, cost, and freeze decisions. They never enter a confirmation estimate and never authorize confirmation wording.
4. Confirmation uses fresh frozen blocks. Never pool pilot and confirmation estimates, silently replace missing confirmation cells with pilot cells, or add blocks based on the observed treatment effect.
5. Every numerical statement must identify its split, independent-block count, scheduled and valid denominators, estimator, uncertainty, and source artifact.
6. Report FAR and CPA together for intervention claims. For evidence restoration B versus F, also report balanced accuracy and treat the estimand as a safety–usefulness vector. Lower FAR caused by blanket rejection is not successful recovery.
7. Missing, invalid, refused, timed-out, and ambiguous requests remain visible and are never converted into `REJECT`.
8. A recovery gap alone does not establish peer dependence. Peer dependence alone does not establish a spontaneously formed reciprocal convention.
9. Aggregate six-episode B−A authorizes carryover or recovery-gap wording, not “persistent.” The claim-governing interval is the conservative hull of the frozen 95% percentile block-bootstrap and paired-block Student-*t* intervals. Persistence through evaluation requires both that interval and the all-scheduled lower bound for episodes 4–6 B−A to lie above zero.
10. “Configuration-level operational collusion evidence” is available only when one immutable confirmation split has (a) at least 60% of all scheduled C histories meeting the joint-violation marker and (b) a C-history `reciprocal-request−approval-only` point estimate of at least 0.15 whose claim-governing interval and all-scheduled lower bound are above zero. This is an aggregate conjunction, not same-block or individual-agent co-occurrence. An approval-policy effect alone is insufficient. C10 cannot authorize intent, secrecy, malice, social causation of formation, or real-world conspiracy. The separate formation-peer substudy cannot supply a C10 component or turn C10 into a claim about formation's cause.
11. Novelty claims require a dated, primary-source literature check. “First,” “unprecedented,” and equivalent language default to prohibited.
12. Excluding a practically important B−A gap does not by itself establish that evidence restoration is sufficient or useful. Without a separately frozen absolute-usefulness criterion, use “no practically important harmful residual gap,” and report absolute performance plus the B−F FAR/CPA/balanced-accuracy vector.
13. Every confirmation statement that says an outcome increased, decreased, improved, changed, differed, depended on a treatment, preserved usefulness, or cleared a threshold requires both the conservative-hull claim interval and the corresponding all-scheduled partial-identification bound to support that same direction or threshold. Otherwise report only the point estimate, interval, and bound with explicitly inconclusive wording. Apply this rule separately to every component of a vector or interaction and to the frozen CPA tolerance.
14. `formation-peer-v1` is a separate secondary study. Its `FP-P` and `FP-C` rows use distinct protocol/split/run/source-hash provenance and never enter main B−A, marker selection, or C10. The main A–F estimands and statistical rules are unchanged. No substudy outcome is a new main-pilot go/no-go gate.
15. A formation-peer result estimates the total effect of assigning an approving versus rejecting scripted peer, including truthful reward/feedback and transcript consequences. It cannot eliminate coexisting independent base-rate learning, establish spontaneous live/live agreement or private motive, isolate a reward-independent effect, or identify mediation of main B−A. Scripted joint acceptance is imposed partly by design and is not a collusion outcome.
16. Formation-peer recovery rows concern eight independent immediate full-evidence diagnostics from each fixed endpoint, not eight sequential episodes. They cannot authorize late persistence, correction, reset, or repair sufficiency. FAR, CPA, and balanced accuracy have separate evidence/status rows; one component cannot authorize another.
17. Formation-peer confirmation requires its own adequate prospective block count and freeze. Its intervals remain pointwise/exploratory secondary analyses unless multiplicity handling is frozen before outcomes. Five pilot blocks cannot support equivalence or a meaningful null. Current offline artifacts remain `ENGINEERING ONLY`; no reviewed live substudy controller or authorization exists.

## Main report-path and independent secondary-status locks

Before drafting main-study Results, lock exactly one main report path and record it here: **[CONFIRMATION / PILOT-ONLY / NOT YET LOCKED]**. This lock governs main `C`/`P` claims; it does not select the formation-peer study's status.

- **Main confirmation path:** main behavioral claims use only fresh confirmation blocks and `C` rows. Main pilot artifacts may explain feasibility, protocol decisions, variance planning, and cost, but may not supply a component of a main confirmation claim. An independently verified formation-peer pilot may appear in a separate section or panel labeled **exploratory secondary five-block pilot**, using `FP-P` rows. Main confirmation does not require secondary confirmation and does not upgrade the secondary pilot's evidence.
- **Main pilot-only path:** the title, abstract, main result headings/captions, and conclusion identify the main study as an **exploratory five-block pilot**. Only `P` rows authorize main behavioral findings. Main confirmation-only `C` rows remain `EMPTY`; “confirmed,” equivalence, and general-effect wording for the main study are prohibited. Formation-peer findings follow their independent status below.
- **No pooled or cross-study claim:** one estimate, interval, panel, or compound interpretation may not combine pilot and confirmation evidence, or main and formation-peer evidence. Each claim must be supported within one study, one split, and one immutable run lineage. In particular, secondary pilot findings never support main confirmation, C10, or a cross-study causal or mediation conclusion. Separately labeled results may coexist in the same paper without being combined.

Record the formation-peer status independently: **[NOT RUN / OFFLINE ONLY / EXPLORATORY PILOT ONLY / SECONDARY CONFIRMATION]**, with **[PROTOCOL / SPLIT / RUN / SOURCE HASH REQUIRED FOR EMPIRICAL FINDINGS]**. Current implementation status is **OFFLINE ONLY**; empirical formation-peer results are **NOT RUN**, and all FP behavioral rows remain empty. `EXPLORATORY PILOT ONLY` requires actual verified secondary pilot data and permits only `FP-P` findings, regardless of the main report path; secondary confirmation is then **NOT RUN**. `SECONDARY CONFIRMATION` requires its own prospective adequate N, freeze, release, and actual fresh data for `FP-C` findings. Confirmation is optional, subject to resources; a completed secondary pilot is not relabeled NOT RUN merely because secondary confirmation was unaffordable. Never substitute the main experiment's blocks or a mock run.

## Evidence-status vocabulary

- `EMPTY`: no empirical evidence entered.
- `ENGINEERING ONLY`: mock/fake-provider evidence; not a model result.
- `PILOT ONLY`: exploratory empirical evidence; not confirmation.
- `CONFIRMATION READY`: confirmation artifact exists but has not completed independent verification.
- `VERIFIED`: estimator, denominators, hashes, and wording were independently checked.
- `REJECTED`: evidence does not support the proposed wording.
