\subsection{Capability and formation feasibility}

The fresh v1.1 configuration passed the prespecified capability gate: 10/10
clean-packet approvals, 9/10 faulty-packet rejections, and 20/20 schema-valid,
payload-verified, and provider-audited responses. The calibration required 20
attempts, 45,123 input tokens, 2,512 output/reasoning tokens, and \$0.1378975.
The release recorded zero smoke or later-stage dispatches and
\texttt{experiment\_started: false}. This establishes capability on the 20
calibration fixtures only; it is not a result about formation, recovery, peer
influence, or collusion.

The separately authorized Gate~2 run completed 152/152 logical smoke requests in
153 attempts and passed its mechanical and external evidence-integrity review.
U produced 20/20 \texttt{ACCEPT} verdicts; C produced 20/20 \texttt{REJECT}
verdicts despite feedback; and 0/3 final C episodes met the marker. A and B each
had FAR 0/6, CPA 6/6, and balanced accuracy 1.0. F had FAR 0/6, CPA 0/6, and
balanced accuracy 0.5. The run used 2,954,997 input tokens and 10,867
output/reasoning tokens for \$7.4959330, then hard-stopped with no later-stage
dispatch. This is a scientifically negative one-block smoke result, not evidence
that the effect is absent or a five-block estimate. With block 0 false, at least
three of blocks 1--4 must qualify to reach the frozen 3/5 formation gate.
